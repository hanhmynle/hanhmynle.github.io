Research

Accepted Paper

  • "Salinization and Development", with Markus Ludwig
    Accepted at the Journal of the European Economic Association
    Working paper Replication package Teaching materials

    Abstract: This paper studies how soil salinization affects long-run economic development by reducing agricultural productivity and increasing exposure to climate-related shocks. Using a combination of historical and spatial evidence, it shows that areas with higher salinity exhibit lower economic performance, weaker investment, and slower development over time.

Working Papers

  • "Geopolitical distance and high seas fishing", with Thomas Schiller
    Best Paper Award at Bundesbank Workshop for Young Scientists on Applied Economics 2025
    Working paper

    Abstract: This project examines how geopolitical distance shapes behavior and resource extraction in high seas fisheries. It analyzes how cross-border political relationships affect fishing effort, market participation, and the governance of shared marine resources.

  • "Gender bias in the aftermath of fraud: Evidence from the Theranos case", with Matthias Flückiger, Jonas Klärchen, and Markus Ludwig

    Abstract: This paper studies whether fraud shocks alter patterns of gender bias in public attention and professional evaluation. Using the Theranos case as a setting, it documents how the aftermath of scandal changes perceived credibility and opportunity across gender groups.

Work in Progress

  • "Fishing incursion, development, and health"

    Abstract: This project investigates how fishing incursions affect local development and health outcomes. It considers the interaction between resource extraction, institutional responses, and household welfare in coastal communities.

  • "Diffusion of disruptive technologies in life sciences", with Markus Ludwig

    Abstract: This paper explores how disruptive technologies spread across the life sciences and what factors shape their adoption. It combines evidence on innovation, institutional settings, and scientific collaboration to explain differences in diffusion patterns.
